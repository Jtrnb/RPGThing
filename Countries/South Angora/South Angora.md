South Angora must remain.. we don't have another option.

South Angora a powerhouse of magic and technology, being one of the firsts to master magical practice and slow the effects of Reclamation. However, during the first instance of the Rupture, the southern half of Angora was not prepared for the fight. Rupture beasts killed a near 30% of South Angora's population, the remaining majority hid in bunkers and factories. Where they lacked in strength they made up for in intellect. Within the first 50 years PR, South Angora had found a way to control spellcasting and drive back Rupture Beasts, even harness their bodies for both Carthyte, Bronze and Brass.

In the coming years South Angora would seat itself as a leading nation in magic and technology, generating energy from boiling water and harnessing the steam. They took claim of the Faultline and used its power harvesting all the Carthyte within and driving back the Rupture Beasts.

What they found at the bottom of the Faultline however, frightened them. A gateway near the bottom that promised no return, only entry for Rupture Beasts. Everything they dropped through never returned, only an empty rope with whisps of Brass and Bronze. 

Now the People of South Angora hold the Faultline, preventing monsters from escaping and keeping the portal below a secret that the general population not need know of. 


### View of Magic
Magic is meant to be used and studied, the better we understand it the better we can make use of it. While no magic seems forbidden there is a system of rules for those who practice it without the precaution of becoming Reclaimed.
