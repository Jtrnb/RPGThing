### Fungalite Features 

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Constitution score increases by 1 and an ability score of your choice increases by 1  
- **Age.** Fungalites reach maturity by age 5 when they stop growing, and typically live to be 260 to 300 years old.  
- **Size.** Fungalites have the an average size of 2 to 10  feet tall with extreme variation of build. 
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet 
- **Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray. 
- **Decomposition.** Your fungal nature grants you the ability to digest dead and undead creatures without harm. 
- **Earthen Roots.**  You have proficiency in the Nature skill. 
- **Languages.** You can speak, read and write Common and Spore Speak. Spore Speak is a very liquid like language consisting of various clicks and open vowels. Those characteristics tie into the general voice of a fungalite. 
### Hard Cap Fungalite 

Hard Caps are stronger than the average fungalite, your skin is tough yet colorful. Hard cap's range from brilliant reds to bright yellows. Their most distinct feature bring the large rounded cap on their head which can be utilized for a variety of things.  

- **Ability Score Increase.**  Your Strength score increases by 1. 
- **Ramming Cap.** Use your broad head to ram into enemies. You can use the shove action as a reaction 
- **Solid Skin.** Your natural armor class is 16.  

### Banded Fungalite 

Banded Fungalites take aspects from toxic mushrooms providing them with potent poison. 

- **Cave Dwellers Night Vision.**  Your cave origins provided you with superior sight in the dark. You can see in dim light within 90 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness within 15 feet you see bright light. Otherwise you see in darkness up to 90 feet as if it were dim light. 
- **Damage Resistances.** poison 
- **Toxic Touch.** as a bonus action you may imbue a melee weapon with poison, your next successful attack with this weapon, the target must make a DC (10 + Constitution) saving throw or become poisoned for 1 minute. You may use this feature once per long rest (twice at level 3 and 3 times at level 6) 

### Surface Fungalite 

Surface Fungalites have adapted to a sunny environment, making them lose most of their natural pigments.  

- **Ability Score Increase.** You can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1 these score may not exceed 20. 
- **Speed.** Your base walking speed is 35 feet. 

### Moonlight Fungalite 

Moonlight Fungalites while being surface bound draw energy from the moon, they thrive during the night some would even say they glow. They are on the paler side with small orbs protruding from their body, these orbs are all sorts of colors and are most noted for their light emitting properties. 

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Intelligence score increases by 1. 
- **Cantrip.** You know the Light cantrip. No components are required for you are a florescent fungalite. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it. 
- **Moon Glow.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight in open moon light. 

### Aroura Fungalite  

These surface dwelling Fungalites are a subspecies of the Banded Fungalite. Aurora Fungalites  while not having poison are highly hallucinogenic when consumed and they utilize this ability and can cast illusions. They, like many, are a very wide range of colors and a most noted for their large eyes which shimmer like glass.  

- **Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma score increases by 1. 
- **Cantrip.** You know the Minor Illusion cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for it. Spell DC is 8 + your Charisma modifier, if your class is a spell class you may use that spell DC instead. 
- **Curios Nature.** You gain one additional language of your choice.
